I am a PhD student in the \href{https://moves.rwth-aachen.de/}{Software Modeling and Verification Group} at RWTH Aachen University. I care about solid foundations for probabilistic verification and bringing theory into practice by developing \href{https://www.caesarverifier.org/}{Caesar}, an automated verification infrastructure for reasoning about software with randomness and proving quantitative correctness properties.

\cvsection{Research}
{\bfseries\color{text}\href{https://www.caesarverifier.org/}{\includegraphics[height=\fontcharht\font`C]{caesar-laurel.pdf}\hspace{0.35em}Caesar verifier}}\hfill{\small\color{accent}main project}\par
Caesar is a deductive verifier for probabilistic programs.
Its core language HeyVL, based on the real-valued logic HeyLo, expresses programs, specifications, and proof rules based on weakest pre-expectation-style semantics.
Caesar uses SMT-based reasoning for verification and also provides a probabilistic model-checking backend for a subset of HeyVL.
